A one-page guide for staff, site reps, and district leaders.

The idea

One page per site that keeps everything together: staff input, meeting notes, and the list we bring to the district. It builds on the Needs / Wants / Wishes / Questions format other sites have used.

  • Staff post needs, wants, wishes, and questions, 👍 the ones they agree with, and read a short summary after every meeting.
  • Reps get the December checklist, posts sorted into "top areas" automatically, and a one-click copy for the district spreadsheet.
  • District leaders can get a read-only view of each site.

Meet & Confer covers working conditions, benefits, and campus needs. It is not a salary-negotiating group.

StaffWhat staff do

Four tabs: Home, Share Your Input, Meeting Notes, What We Heard.

  1. Open the link and sign inwith your district Google account. Home shows what Meet & Confer is, the four December steps (the current one is outlined in red), your rep, and the site meeting time.
  2. Share Your InputPick a column: Needs to do your job well, Wants that would make work better, Wishes for bigger ideas, or Questions for the district. Click + Add, write a short title and any details, and click Post. Check Post anonymously if you'd like.
  3. 👍 instead of repeatingIf you start typing something that's already posted, the hub shows "Already posted?" Click 👍 on it instead. The most-agreed posts rise to the top.
  4. CommentClick 💬 on any post to add a comment, anonymously if you like.
  5. Meeting NotesAfter each monthly meeting, read the quick summary. Open "Full notes" or the recording for details.
  6. What We HeardBefore the list goes to the district, check that nothing was missed. It also shows the top areas so far. If something's missing, add it before the deadline.
Once your rep sorts a post, you'll see a label such as Going to M&C, Referred to site (handled by our principal or department), or Answered, with a short note.

Reps see a fifth tab, Rep Page, on the same link.

Top areas are automatic. Each post is sorted into an area such as Time & schedules, Benefits & leave, or Facilities, using its words, and areas are ranked by posts + 👍, so the group's priorities are easy to see.

DistrictWhat district leaders see

The same four tabs plus a read-only Site Summary: totals, the top areas, and every post with its type, status, and 👍 count, including posts a rep has hidden. District viewers can't change statuses, hide posts, or edit notes.

Privacy, in plain terms

  • Only district Google accounts can open a site's hub.
  • Anonymous posts store no name or email, so even the rep can't see who wrote them.
  • 👍 votes are anonymous too. The hub keeps only the count.
  • Hidden posts stay in the record but no longer show to staff.

Using the guided demo

  • The main link plays by itself: the staff side, then the rep and district side, then it starts over.
  • ⏸ Pause / ▶ Play stop and resume. ◀ Back and Next ▶ move one step at a time.
  • Speed: choose Slow, Normal, or Fast. Each step stays up long enough to read its caption.
  • 🔊 Narration reads each step aloud using your browser's built-in voice. Browsers only allow sound after a click, so turn it on yourself.
  • Choose a tour to play only the staff side or only the rep side. Explore it yourself opens the demo with a Staff / Rep / District switcher.
  • Everything in the demo is sample data that stays in your browser.
