Everything for Meet & Confer, in one place.

Each site gets one page. Staff post needs, wants, wishes, and questions, 👍 what they agree with, and read a quick summary of every meeting. Reps get a checklist, automatic "top areas," and a one-click copy for the district spreadsheet.

For staffThe Needs / Wants / Wishes / Questions columns other sites have used, plus an anonymous option, a duplicate check, and a view of where their input went.
For repsEvery December task in one place: intro email, survey, share-back, and the district spreadsheet.
For the districtA read-only summary of each site, ranked by what staff care about most.
